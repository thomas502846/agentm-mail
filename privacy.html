Privacy policy

agentm-mail reads only its owner's own Gmail, with the read-only scope gmail.readonly, to list emails awaiting a reply. Data and tokens stay on the owner's computer. Nothing is shared, sold, transferred, used for advertising or used to train AI models. Use of data from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
